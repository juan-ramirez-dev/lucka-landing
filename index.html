The first time you open it

Your system will warn you. That’s normal.

Lucka is open source and doesn't pay for Apple or Microsoft certificates. So your system asks you to confirm once.

macOS

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Lucka to Applications.
  2. Open it. A warning shows up: click Done.
  3. System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
If you’re technical 
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Lucka.app

Windows

  1. Open the installer.
  2. “Windows protected your PC” shows up: click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway.

After that it updates itself. For voice, it asks for microphone permission once.
